Privacy Policy

Pengolo for iPhone and iPad · Effective 17 November 2026

In short
  • There is no account and no sign-up. We run no servers and receive none of your data.
  • Your progress and coins are saved on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads. You are asked before any tracking, and you can say no.

Who we are

Pengolo is made by Serhat Önal, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: serhaton@gmail.com.

What the game stores, and where

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or anything you type. The game has no chat and no free-text input.

Services from Apple

  • iCloud keeps your progress in sync across your devices and restores it after reinstalling. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Game Center is optional. If you sign in, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown under your Game Center nickname. Coins and progress are never stored in Game Center.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

The game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob: rewarded videos, which only play when you choose to watch one, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. There are no banner ads. Buying "No Ads" or the Welcome Pack removes the full-screen ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be derived;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data.

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", or turn off tracking in iOS Settings, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your activity in other companies' apps and websites. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), Google's consent message asks for your choices before any ads are requested. Details: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it, because we do not hold any. The result card you can share after a level is created on your device and goes only where you send it through the iOS share sheet.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (where applicable): you can change your choices at any time under Settings → Ad choices in the game, which reopens Google's consent message.
  • Delete game data: delete the app to remove data from the device. To remove the iCloud copy: iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Pengolo → Delete Data.
  • Game Center: sign out or manage your profile in iOS Settings → Game Center.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google; we will gladly help you find the right place.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
