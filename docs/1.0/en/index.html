One tap, one glide

Penguins only stop when they hit something. The puzzle is the order: who moves first, who waits, who becomes the wall someone else needs.

Cross Antarctica

Three adventures, thirty puzzles each. Every adventure brings a new rule, shown in a few seconds without a single line of text.

Ice that holds only once

Cracked ice turns to water behind you. Sometimes it is a trap. Sometimes breaking it on purpose is the whole trick.

Wake the sleepy ones

A sleeping penguin is just an obstacle until somebody bumps into it. Then it has a home to reach too.

Duel on one phone

Play a friend face to face on the same device, or take on the computer at three levels. First to three games wins.

Make the penguin yours

Hats, hair, glasses and a very loud rainbow scarf, all earned with coins from playing. Everything is cosmetic; nothing buys an advantage.
