How it plays

One tap, one glide

Penguins only stop when they bump into something. The puzzle is the order: who goes first, who waits, and who becomes the wall someone else needs.

Step by step across the map

Every continent is a map, every map has dozens of stops. Each episode adds one new rule, shown in a few seconds without a line of text.

Ice that holds only once

Cracked ice turns to water behind you. Sometimes it is a trap. Sometimes breaking it on purpose is the whole trick.

Wake up the sleepy ones

A sleeping penguin is just an obstacle until someone bumps into it. Once awake, it has a home of its own to reach.

Duels on one phone

Play a friend face to face on the same device, or take on the computer at three difficulty levels. First to three wins.

Make the penguin yours

Hats, hair, glasses and a very showy rainbow scarf, all bought with coins you earn by playing. Everything is cosmetic; nothing buys an advantage.

The second theme

Northern lights after dark

When the sun goes down, Pengolo follows: a deep blue sky, green and purple lights, and quiet falling snow. The theme follows your phone’s setting, or you can keep it on night or day all the time.
