Questions and answers

How do I play?

Tap a penguin and it slides in the direction of its arrow until it hits a wall, a rock, water, another penguin or its own igloo. Get every penguin into the igloo of its colour. Fewer moves earn more stars.

I am stuck. What now?

You cannot lose. Use Undo (three free per level) or Restart. If the level can no longer be finished, the game tells you. The light bulb shows the next good move for coins or a short video.

How does the daily puzzle work?

One new puzzle a day, played once. Solving it earns 50 coins and extends your streak. Miss a day and the streak starts again.

What is the lucky wheel?

On the Daily page you can spin the wheel once a day. Most spins give a few coins; now and then a big coin prize or an outfit comes up. If a video is available you can choose to watch it for one more spin that day. The wheel cannot be bought or spun with coins.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone or iPad?

Yes, if iCloud is on for the game (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud). Progress, coins and outfits come back by themselves. Settings → iCloud in the game shows whether backup is active.

A purchase did not arrive, or I changed devices.

Open the Shop and tap "Restore purchases". Coin packs are delivered once and travel with your iCloud progress; the Welcome Pack, Aurora Bundle and No Ads are restored from your Apple account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I turn off ads?

Rewarded videos only play when you ask for one. Buying "No Ads" or the Welcome Pack removes the full-screen ads between levels.

Do outfits change the game?

No. Hats, hair, glasses and scarves are cosmetic only. Nothing you can buy gives an advantage.

How does the duel work?

Two players share one device, or you play the computer at Easy, Medium or Hard. Players take turns moving their own penguins; the first to win three games takes the duel.

Game Center does not sign in.

Sign in first in iOS Settings → Game Center, then open the game again. Game Center is optional; the game works fully without it.
