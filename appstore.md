Pengolo is a cosy puzzle game on ice. Tap a penguin and it glides until something stops it: a rock, the edge of the ice, or another penguin. Your job is to get every penguin back to its igloo.

It sounds simple, and the first levels are. Then you notice the real puzzle is the order. Who goes first? Who waits? And which penguin has to become the wall that someone else needs?

FIRST ANTARCTICA, THEN THE WHOLE WORLD
The journey starts on the ice with 90 hand-tuned puzzles across three episodes. Antarctica is only the first stop: future updates will take the penguins to new continents, each with its own scenery and its own kind of puzzle.

RULES YOU LEARN BY PLAYING
• Slippery ice: penguins only stop when they bump into something.
• Cracked ice: it holds once, then turns to water behind you. Sometimes a trap, sometimes the whole trick.

Every new rule is shown in a few seconds, without a line of text.

A PUZZLE EVERY DAY
A fresh daily puzzle, a streak to keep alive and a gift waiting each day you come back.

DUELS ON ONE DEVICE
Play a friend face to face on the same iPhone or iPad, or take on the computer at three difficulty levels. First to three wins takes the duel.

MAKE THE PENGUIN YOURS

DAY AND NIGHT
A bright day theme and a night theme with northern lights. The game follows your device setting, or you can pick one.

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Plays offline.
• No timers and no lives in the adventure: think as long as you like.

• Larger symbols option, so colour is never the only cue.
• Designed for both iPhone and iPad.
• Progress syncs across your devices with iCloud.
• Game Center leaderboards and achievements.

Pengolo is free to play. It contains optional in-app purchases and shows ads; a one-time purchase removes the ads between levels.
